How this portfolio handles the information you choose to share, and recognition for the tools that help power the experience.

Privacy at a glance

This is a personal portfolio. It does not include advertising pixels or first-party visitor analytics. Opening the contact questionnaire does not submit your information to a server. When you finish, your email application opens a prepared draft; you decide whether to send it.

What is stored locally

The site uses local storage to remember your light or dark preference (elyas-reference-mode) and session storage to avoid replaying the full introduction on every page load (elyas-visited-once). You can delete both through your browser’s site-data settings.

Third-party resources

The current preview loads the Onest and EB Garamond fonts through Google Fonts and can load Lenis smooth-scrolling JavaScript from jsDelivr when supported. These providers may receive ordinary request information as part of delivering their files. Other than what the host needs to serve the site, no visitor details are intentionally transmitted by this site's application code.

Contact
